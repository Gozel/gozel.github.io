The Envirofy Project

Envirofy is the first science-based eco-friendly web browser extension for online shoppers. The Envirofy addon is part of a collaborative research project from the Univeristy of Glasgow and the Univeristy of Bristol. The goal of our research is to determine which behaviour change techniques are the most effective in supporting online shoppers to buy eco-friendly food products.
